Sunvia Privacy Policy

Sunvia is a local photography companion for sunset framing notes, exposure logs, shoot checklists, and optional gift unlocks. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information on your device.

Information stored on your device

  • Quick Login account identifiers and passwords generated and stored locally for demo access.
  • Shoot session records such as spot aliases, direction notes, shutter, aperture, ISO, text notes, lifecycle state, and timestamps.
  • Optional photos you capture with the camera or select from Photos for session references or a profile avatar.
  • Optional speech-to-text results that you convert into session notes.
  • Studio checklist progress, favorite spots, and prep timer preferences.
  • Local records of gift pack purchases completed through Apple In-App Purchase.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose Take Photo or update a profile photo.
  • Microphone: used only when you start a voice note or speech-to-text action.
  • Photos: used only when you choose images from your library or save a cover image.
  • Speech Recognition: used only when you convert speech into text notes.
  • Notifications: a system permission prompt may appear once. Sunvia A-side does not schedule marketing pushes and does not take over push delivery delegates.

Sunvia does not use App Tracking Transparency or advertising identifiers for cross-app tracking.

Network activity

Core logging works offline. Opening Terms of Use or Privacy Policy loads these pages inside the app. In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. Sunvia does not operate a cloud account backend for session sync in this version.

Sharing

We do not sell your session notes or photos. Content leaves the device only if you export or share it yourself, or when Apple processes a purchase.

Retention and deletion

Data remains in the app sandbox until you clear sessions, log out and clear local content, delete media, or uninstall Sunvia. Clearing sessions removes local shoot records from this device.

Children

Sunvia is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions about this build, use the publisher support channel configured for the App Store listing.
