Sunvia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Sunvia is a photography companion for golden hour and blue hour. It offers a Golden Feed of sample creator posts, a Framing viewfinder with a solar ephemeris HUD, an Exposure Journal, and a Creator Studio with optional Pro plans. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

  • Quick Login account identifiers and passwords generated and stored locally.
  • Journal logs such as spot aliases, direction notes, shutter, aperture, ISO, EV values, text notes, lifecycle state, and timestamps.
  • Optional photos you capture with the camera or select from Photos for log references or your profile photo.
  • Optional speech-to-text results that you convert into log notes.
  • Studio preferences, such as Twilight Ephemeris Alerts on or off, and local records of Pro plan purchases completed through Apple In-App Purchase.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you tap the shutter, Take Photo, or the Audio & Optical Sensors check.
  • Microphone: requested only when you start a voice note or the sensor check.
  • Photos: requested only when you choose images from your library, such as a profile photo.
  • Speech Recognition: requested only when you convert speech into text notes.
  • Notifications: a system permission prompt appears once after launch. Sunvia does not send marketing pushes.
  • App Tracking Transparency: a system prompt appears once after launch so you can choose whether apps may track you. Sunvia does not read the advertising identifier, does not combine data with third-party data for advertising, and works the same whichever option you choose.

Network activity

Core logging works offline. Opening the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use loads these pages from the web. In-App Purchases are processed by Apple. Sunvia does not operate a cloud account backend for syncing logs in this version.

Sharing

We do not sell your logs or photos. Content leaves the device only if you export or share it yourself, for example with Export EXIF Card, or when Apple processes a purchase.

Retention and deletion

Data remains in the app sandbox until you delete your account from Studio, delete media, or uninstall Sunvia. Delete Account removes local logs, photos, profile photo, preferences, and local purchase records from this device.

Children

Sunvia is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the publisher support channel listed with the App Store listing.
